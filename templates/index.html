Why ISL is easy to start
  • 🖐️ No pens, no paper — your hands are the language.
  • 👀 Half the meaning is in your face — eyebrows ask questions, mouth shows feelings.
  • 🧩 ISL grammar is simple: Subject – Object – Verb. “Me water want” = “I want water.”
  • ⏱️ 10 minutes a day is enough to hold a basic conversation in a month.

💡 5 rules that make learning ISL easy

  • 1. Face first. In ISL your eyebrows, mouth and head carry grammar — a raised eyebrow turns a sentence into a question.
  • 2. Word order: Subject → Object → Verb. Say “You food want?”, not “Do you want food?”. English is rearranged by your listener.
  • 3. No small words. ISL skips “is/am/are/the”. “Me student” = “I am a student.”
  • 4. Copy the movement, not just the hand. Every sign has a handshape + where + how it moves. Practice in slow-mo here.
  • 5. Five signs a day. Tiny daily practice beats weekend cramming — use the quiz to test yourself.
